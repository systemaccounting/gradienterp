You are purchasing a member account in the gradientERP AWS organization. gradientERP provisions your
AWS account with an AgentCore-administered ERP application and enables its communication with other
accounts through an event bus hosted in the operator account. You will receive a monthly invoice for
your AWS account use. gradientERP adds a 20% operator markup to your monthly AWS bill for managing
infrastructure and code. You may cancel anytime by closing the account. Your books and documents are
exported to you at closure and stay downloadable for 15 days. The ERP application and everything
running in your AWS account are destroyed at once and cannot be brought back. After the 15 days the
export is deleted and the AWS account is closed.
