# Privacy Notice

This notice covers the personal data gradientERP (gradienterp.cloud) holds about the people who
hold an account: what it is, why, where, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and your rights.
For the data a business puts in its own ERP instance — its employees, customers, vendors — the
business decides, and gradientERP processes it for them under the [Data Processing
Agreement](DPA.md).

## What we hold

- your login email
- your private account record: name, phone, address
- for each ERP instance you create, the business's legal profile: name, address, email, phone and
  tax id
- the Stripe customer and payment-method ids for the cards you save — the card itself is held by
  Stripe, never by us
- which version of the terms you accepted, and when
- records of the hosting invoices billed to you and their payment
- request logs: IP address, time and the request made, for security

All of it comes from you, from the business you act for, or from Stripe about your payments. Sign-in
tokens are kept in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

The email, the account record, the legal profile and a saved card are required to hold an account
and create an instance — without them the contract cannot be performed. Nothing else is required.

## Why, and on what basis

| use | legal basis |
|---|---|
| running your account and your instances | performance of the contract |
| billing and collecting the hosting invoice | performance of the contract |
| keeping invoices and accounting records | legal obligation (tax and accounting law) |
| refusing a new account to someone who left a balance unpaid | legitimate interest |
| security and abuse logs | legitimate interest |

No decision with legal or similarly significant effect about you is made by automated means alone.

## Where

Account records are stored in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1). Each instance runs
in the AWS region its owner picks. Where your data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the
transfer relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK Addendum and
the Swiss amendments), and a copy is available on request through the support form.

## Who else handles it

- Amazon Web Services — hosting, storage, email (Amazon SES), and the agent's model (Amazon Bedrock)
- Stripe — payments

Each is bound to process the data only to provide its service. We do not sell or share personal
information for advertising. What a business chooses to publish as openly operated is public by its
choice; your private record and the legal profile are never published.

## How long

For as long as your account exists. When you delete it, you are erased from our records: your name,
email, phone and address are removed. What stays:

- **accounting records.** Invoices and the entries that record them keep their amounts, dates and
  ids for the period tax and accounting law requires (commonly seven to ten years), and a balance is
  computed from that history — removing an entry would change every balance after it. The entries
  name an erased record, not you (GDPR Article 17(3)(b) and (e)).
- **non-payment records.** One-way hashes of the email and phone, card fingerprints, how each
  instance ended and any balance owed — naming nobody, kept against non-payment while a balance is
  owed and for the limitation period for recovering it.
- **request logs** are kept for 90 days.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data (every instance exports its records), correct it (Info &
Billing), have it erased as described above, restrict or object to a use based on legitimate
interest, and receive it in a portable form. You can also complain to your data protection
supervisory authority. Requests go through https://gradienterp.cloud/support.
